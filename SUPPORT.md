## Funded direction

To request more work in a specific direction, open a **Funded direction** issue and include a confirmed public transaction hash. The first issue that cites a valid, previously unclaimed transaction receives operational attribution for that transaction.

A transaction hash is public chain evidence, not proof that the issue author owns the sending wallet. A hash cannot be reused. A donation can express a requested direction, but it does not purchase support, ownership, returns, a deadline, priority, acceptance, or work outside the project's safety boundaries.

Never post a private key, seed phrase, exchange login, wallet backup, or other secret.
